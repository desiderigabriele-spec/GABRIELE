# Prezzi di mercato dei siti web: Milano e Italia (2025-2026), per valutare il listino Impulso / Presenza / Signature

> **How these notes were made (read first).** The web-fetch tool and direct curl were **blocked by the session's egress proxy** (DNS failure, then 403 "connect_rejected" on every Italian site tried: weareblog.it, webcomconsulting.it, wedodigital.it, prontopro.it, preventivi.it, getresponse.com, polonord.studio). So **every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from a full read of them**. Where the search extract tied a number clearly to one URL, it is cited to that URL. Where it gave a number without saying which page it came from, it is marked **[attribution uncertain]**. Before you publish any of these numbers as quotes, open the URLs to check them.
> Almost all sources are **agencies or freelancers selling the service** (a commercial bias), or marketplaces whose ranges mix very small jobs with large ones. Unless stated otherwise, Italian agency price lists are **net of 22% IVA** ([WeAreBlog](https://www.weareblog.it/quanto-costa-un-sito-web/)).

## 1. Published price ranges: landing page, sito vetrina (5-7 pages), custom/premium, 3D/immersive. Milan vs Italy, freelance vs agency

### Takeaway
In Milan, agencies quote roughly **€800-2,500 for a custom landing page with copy and tracking**, **€1,800-4,500 for a sito vetrina**, and **€4,000-10,000 for a structured custom site**. A real 3D/WebGL site from a specialist studio usually starts in the **tens of thousands of euros**. Across Italy, freelancers price well below this: the median starting price of eight published price lists for a vetrina is €1,100. **Impulso (€1,500) is mid-market for Milan. Presenza (€2,900) sits in the middle of the Milan vetrina band and below it once its included extras are counted. Signature (€7,500) is low for a site with 3D, visual identity and strategy.**

### Inferences
- **Impulso at €1,500** (1 page, copy, animations, form to phone/Sheets/WhatsApp, analytics and Meta Pixel, 7-10 days): in the middle of the Milan custom landing page band of €800-1,800 / €800-2,500 (Webcom). It matches GetResponse's agency figure (€500-1,500 for basic projects) and is below its custom-coded figure (€2,000-3,000). It is above the typical freelance range (€300-1,500). The price is consistent with the market and could be defended at up to about €1,800-2,000 for a code-built page with copy included.
- **Presenza at €2,900** (5-7 custom pages, copy, local SEO, real-time enquiries, Google Business Profile, cookie/privacy compliance): inside the Milan band (Webcom €1,800-4,500) and just below the WeDoDigital average (€3,000-4,000). It is well above the national median starting price (€1,100, WeAreBlog), but those price lists rarely include copy or Google Business Profile. Valuing the copy separately at €150-400 per page (section 2) gives about €900-2,400 for 6 pages, plus about €300-1,500 for Google Business Profile setup. **€2,900 is therefore competitive, and arguably below market, for what it includes.**
- **Signature at €7,500** (3D scene, signature animations, visual identity, strategy, score >90): close to the Milan "custom company site" band of €4,000-10,000, which **does not include 3D or brand identity**. Brand identity alone costs about €1,500-8,000 (section 2), and "light 3D integration" alone costs about $3,500-12,000 (Metabole). Against specialist studios (WebGL from €25k), Signature is **heavily underpriced**. It is credible as a "small studio" entry ticket, but there is room to raise it to €9,000-12,000, or to define the 3D scope precisely ("one hero scene", not a 3D environment).
- Freelance vs agency: freelancers are typically 40-60% cheaper than agencies at the same tier (Webcom: vetrina from €800-1,500 freelance vs €1,800-3,000 agency; ikwaba: €1,000-3,000 vs €3,000-8,000).

### Gaps
- Assintel and the Osservatori (Politecnico di Milano) publish **no survey of website build prices**. None was found.
- No Milan studio with Awwwards credentials publishes prices: the premium tier rests only on Clutch minimums and on Metabole, a European rather than Italian source.
- Full pages could not be checked (fetching was blocked). Several attributions are marked uncertain.

## 2. Prices of the extras: extra page, translation / extra language, SEO copy per page, logo / brand identity, blog setup, copywriting per page

### Takeaway
Most of the studio's extras sit **inside or below** the market ranges found. Extra page (€290), SEO copy per page (€190) and blog (€690) are in line with the market. Extra language (€490) and logo plus visual identity (€850) are **at the low end**, especially for "visual identity". Swiss local SEO (€590) is in line with one-off Google Business Profile setup. No market data was found for AI photos/video (€390).

### Inferences
- **Extra page €290**: in the middle of the €150-400 marginal range. Consistent.
- **Extra language €490**: Marketrock's breakdown (translation at €20-50 per page, technical setup €500-1,500 per language) puts a 6-page language at about €620-1,800+. €490 is **below** that unless translation is AI-assisted with light review. Consider €590-790, or make clear that it covers technical setup plus reviewed translation of up to N pages.
- **SEO copy per page €190**: in the middle of the per-page range (€100-200 senior per Copywriterblog, €150-400 per De Matteis) and above SEO articles (€80-250). Consistent.
- **Logo and visual identity €850**: in line with a freelance logo (€500-1,500, the "median €890" is unverified) but **low for "visual identity"** (from €1,500). Either rename it "Logo e kit base" or raise it to €1,200-1,500 if it includes palette, typography and guidelines.
- **Blog €690**: for setting up a blog section on an existing site, this is in line with the market (comparable data is mostly blog sites at €1,600+). It does not include articles (€80-250 each).
- **Swiss local SEO €590**: in line with the one-off Italian range (€500-1,500). It may be low for Switzerland (see section 3).
- **AI photos/video €390**: no comparable Italian data found.

### Gaps
- No Italian market data on prices for AI-generated photos/video for websites.
- No standardised "blog setup" item found in Milan agency price lists.
- No national copywriter rate survey (for example an association survey) was found. Every rate comes from individual professionals.

## 3. Milan vs national average (evidence), Ticino / Switzerland

### Takeaway
**No reliable numerical comparison** between Milan and the Italian average was found. Milan sources (Webcom, WeDoDigital) quote vetrina sites at about €1,800-4,500, against a national median starting price of €1,100 for published price lists. On ProntoPro, however, Milan and national ranges look almost identical. Ticino/Switzerland is clearly more expensive: a 4-5 page site in Lugano costs about CHF 3,000, and Swiss SMEs typically pay CHF 3,500-15,000.

### Inferences
- Agency sources suggest Milan sits in the upper range of Italian prices, with vetrina sites at about 1.5-3x the national median starting price. The comparison is biased, though: Milan agencies against national price lists that include freelancers. Marketplace data (ProntoPro) shows no clear Milan premium. Treat any "Milan premium" claim as **unproven**.
- For Ticino clients, the studio's prices (Impulso €1,500 against a CHF 1,400 landing page in Lugano; Presenza €2,900 against CHF 3,000-3,200) are **roughly level with or below the Lugano market**. The "SEO locale Svizzera" extra at €590 could therefore be raised.
