# Prezzi di mercato 2025-2026: manutenzione siti, hosting e crescita continua (SEO, contenuti, CRO), Milano e Italia

Scope: fact base for judging three proposed monthly plans from a small Milan studio. Cura 59 €/mese (fast hosting, backups, security and checks, updates, uptime monitoring). Cura+ 149 €/mese (Cura plus 1 hour of changes per month and a monthly report). Crescita 390 €/mese (Cura+ plus conversion tests, continuous SEO and 2 new content pieces per month).

**Method note, please read.** In this session WebFetch failed with DNS errors on every domain tried (siteground.com, aruba.it, serverplan.com, vercel.com, netlify.com). curl through the proxy was also refused (403, connect_rejected). **No page was opened directly.** Every figure below comes from WebSearch result summaries and snippets, each tied to the URL that the summary attributed it to. Where a summary mixed several sources without saying clearly which figure came from which page, the figure is marked "(attribuzione incerta)". Nearly all sources are commercial pages from agencies, freelancers or hosting affiliates, so read them as list prices or positioning, not as market statistics. IVA: wherever a source states it, it is noted. Suppliers in the regime forfettario invoice without IVA, so their price is the final one. Others usually show prices "+ IVA" (22%).

## 1. Published prices of website maintenance plans in Italy, and what they include

### Takeaway
Published maintenance-only plans (updates, backups, security, monitoring, usually **without hosting**) cluster at **30–60 €/month**. Plans that add change hours or "modifiche illimitate" and a report reach **59–150 €/month**, and premium tiers go up to about 250–270 €/month. Cura at 59 € with hosting included sits in the mid-market. Cura+ at 149 € with only 1 hour of changes sits at the high end of what is published.

### Inferences
- **Cura 59 €/month** (hosting included) compares with maintenance-only plans at 30–50 € plus hosting at roughly 4–35 €/month (see section 2). That puts it in the middle of the market and is defensible, especially if the hosting is genuinely faster than shared hosting. Kauky (Milan) starts at 30 € for maintenance only.
- **Cura+ 149 €/month** sits at the top of the published "maintenance + small changes" tier. Luigi Maronese charges 145 € and Teutra's e-commerce range ends at 150 €. Its weakness is that WP-OK and WP In Progress advertise *unlimited* changes at 59–119 €/month, and Nestweb's 1–2 h/month benchmark works out to about 42–67 €/month. The price gap between Cura and Cura+ is **90 €/month for 1 hour plus a report**. That is above the 40–100 €/hour freelance rates (section 4), so it will be visible to anyone who compares.
- Plans that include change hours almost never publish their price (Nextastudio's 2 h and 5 h tiers are "su misura"). The direct comparables are therefore few.

### Gaps
- No page could be opened to confirm the current price, IVA treatment, or exactly what each plan includes (hosting yes or no, backup frequency).
- No published Milan-based plan with "1 hour of changes + report" at a stated price was found.
- No non-WordPress plans were found (for example maintenance for static, Astro or Next.js sites), and no aggregated survey (association or Osservatorio) of maintenance fees.

## 2. Hosting costs: Italian providers, managed hosting, Vercel and Netlify

### Takeaway
Italian shared and WordPress hosting **renews at about 50–90 € + IVA per year** (≈4–7 €/month), after very low first-year promotions. SiteGround renews at **16–40 €/month**. Managed WordPress hosting (Kinsta) starts at **$35/month**. Vercel Pro and Netlify Pro cost about **$20/month**. Pure hosting cost is therefore a small part of a 59 € Cura plan, unless the studio uses premium managed hosting.

- **Aruba**: Hosting per WordPress base costs **11,90 € + IVA in year one** and renews at **49,99 € + IVA/year**. Hosting Gestito per WordPress costs **14,90 € + IVA** in year one and renews at **79,00 € + IVA/year** (another article says 79,90 €). Hyper Hosting Gestito WordPress costs **199 € + IVA** in year one and renews at **420 € + IVA/year**. Figures come from third-party review and affiliate sites — [SupportHost, Rinnovo Aruba 2026](https://supporthost.com/it/rinnovo-aruba/); [Agenda Digitale, Aruba hosting gestito WordPress](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/cultura-digitale/aruba-hosting-gestito-wordpress-intelligenza-artificiale-performance/); [Cyber Security 360, Aruba Hyper Hosting](https://www.cybersecurity360.it/cultura-cyber/aruba-hyper-hosting-architettura-cloud-sicurezza-performance/) (which source gives which tier is uncertain)

### Inferences
- If Cura runs on Italian shared or managed hosting (≈50–90 €/year + IVA), hosting costs the studio **about 4–8 €/month per client**. The remaining ~50 € pays for the service (updates, checks, monitoring). On SiteGround GrowBig at renewal (28 €/month) or Kinsta ($35/month), the hosting margin almost disappears unless several sites share one plan.
- For static or Jamstack sites on Vercel or Netlify, a single Pro account (~$20/month) can host many client sites. The per-client hosting cost is then close to zero, and "fast hosting" becomes mainly a value claim.

### Gaps
- No official pricing page could be opened (DNS and proxy blocks), so no figure here was checked against the official site. Aruba and SiteGround figures come from affiliate reviews.
- Vercel Hobby terms (non-commercial use only) were not checked in this session. Vercel and Netlify prices are in USD with no EU VAT detail.

## 3. Monthly SEO retainers (Italy and Milan), cost per blog article, and CRO/A-B testing retainers

### Takeaway
Italian **local SEO** retainers are commonly quoted at **300–1.500 €/month**. A Milan local SEO page quotes **450–950 €/month**, and several sources say that **under 300–500 €/month** SEO has little effect. An SEO-optimised **1,000-word article** costs about **80–250 €** (basic articles 25–60 €). **CRO retainers** with continuous testing are quoted at **1.500–4.000 €/month** (one agency source). Crescita's 390 € covers SEO, 2 articles and CRO, which is **well below** what these components cost when bought separately.

- Semrush agency directory (self-declared, USD, **project** budgets not monthly fees): one Milan agency lists $2,500–10,000, one Monza agency $0–2,500 — [Semrush Agency Partners, Lombardia](https://it.semrush.com/agencies/list/seo-consulting/lombardy)

**CRO / A-B testing**

- An A/B test typically needs **2–4 weeks** to reach statistical significance, depending on traffic — [Eskimoz, CRO agency](https://www.eskimoz.co.uk/cro-agency/)
- Boraso, Cerved and 2open offer CRO/A-B testing but publish no prices — [Boraso](https://www.boraso.com/marketing/cro-conversion-optimization/); [Cerved](https://www.cerved.com/pro-web-digital-consulting/cro-ux); [2open](https://www.2open.it/consulenza-cro.html)

### Inferences
- **Crescita 390 €/month, broken into market parts:** Cura+ (149 €) leaves **241 €/month** for continuous SEO, 2 content pieces and conversion tests. At market rates, 2 SEO articles alone cost 160–500 € (80–250 € each). Local SEO alone starts at 300–500 €/month (450–950 € in Milan). Agency CRO retainers start at 1.500 €/month. Bought separately, the bundle would cost **well over 1.000 €/month**.
- So 390 € reads either as **very aggressive or underpriced**, or as a "light" scope. The light reading would be: GBP upkeep and on-page SEO, 2 short articles of 600–800 words, and one simple conversion test per month or quarter on low traffic. Given the 2–4 week test duration and the low traffic of small sites, a monthly "conversion test" claim may be hard to deliver with statistical significance.
- Several SEO sources say that budgets under 300–500 €/month for SEO alone are ineffective. A buyer who has read these guides may see 390 € all-in as either a bargain or a warning sign. Clear scope definitions (word counts, number of tests, what "continuous SEO" means) would help.
- A plausible market-aligned repositioning, as an inference only: about **590–890 €/month** for Cura+ plus local SEO plus 2 articles, with "conversion tests" framed as monthly CRO checks or heatmap reviews rather than formal A/B tests. Alternatively, keep 390 € and narrow the scope explicitly.

### Gaps
- No Milan-specific blog article prices, and no survey (for example an association price list) on copywriter rates for 2025-2026.
- Only one Italian source publishes a CRO retainer price. No CRO price aimed at small businesses or micro-sites was found.
- Most SEO pages were seen only through search summaries, and some figures could not be tied to a single page.

## 4. Hourly rates for small site changes (freelance web developer and agency) in Italy

### Takeaway
Italian freelance web developers typically charge **30–60 €/hour (junior to mid)** and **60–100 €/hour (senior)**. Agencies and maintenance plans bill extra work at around **100 €/hour**. Cura+'s implied price for its included hour (149 − 59 = 90 €, including the report) is in line with agency rates and above freelance rates.

### Cited Findings
- Freelance programmer rates in 2026 range **25–120 €/hour**, with senior full-stack around **70–90 €/hour** — [SystemForge, Tariffe programmatore freelance 2026](https://systemforge.it/blog/tariffe-programmatore-freelance-italia-2026/) (attribuzione incerta between SystemForge and freelancedev.it)
- Web developer **junior 30–50 €/hour, senior 60–100 €/hour** (2024-2025 comparison) — [Tattà Tools, calcolatore tariffa freelance](https://tattaos.com/strumenti/freelance-rate) (attribuzione incerta)
- Web/app development **40–80 €/hour** (senior 80–150 €/hour), with 2024 data — [Pasquale Pillitteri, Tariffe freelance ICT Italia 2026](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/454/ict-freelance-rates-italy) (attribuzione incerta)
- By experience: **junior 20–35 €/hour, mid 35–55 €/hour, senior 55–90 €/hour**, cloud/AI/security specialists 80–150+ €/hour. A forfettario freelancer earning 30.000 € net needs a **minimum of about 41 €/hour**, since only 60–70% of hours are billable — [freelancedev.it, Sviluppatore freelance in Italia 2026](https://freelancedev.it/guide-sviluppatori-freelance/sviluppatore-freelance-in-italia); [freelancedev.it, Quanto guadagna](https://freelancedev.it/blog/quanto-guadagna-uno-sviluppatore-freelance) (attribuzione incerta)
- Maintenance supplier rate for extra work: **100 €/hour**, IVA-free final price with the 4% fund included — [Milo Web App](https://www.milowebapp.it/assistenza-siti-web/)
- International agency on Italian pages: **$39/hour** emergency support, **$129/hour** custom development — [Seahawk](https://seahawkmedia.com/it/wordpress/piano-di-assistenza-del-sito-web/)
- SEO consulting by the hour: **80–150 €/hour freelance, 150–250 €/hour senior or agency** — (2026 Italian SEO guides, see section 3; attribuzione incerta)

### Inferences
- Pricing the included hour in Cura+ at 90 € (minus the report) is in line with the agency benchmark of about 100 €/hour. Next to 40–60 € freelance rates and competitors' "unlimited changes" at 59–119 €/month, a 1-hour cap looks thin. Options: include 2 hours, allow unused hours to roll over, or keep 149 € and add faster response or priority support.
- An explicit rate for extra hours beyond the plan, for example 60–90 €/hour, would sit inside the market range and support the Cura+ upsell.

### Gaps
- No Milan-specific hourly rate survey for web developers was found. Sources are national guides and calculators, mostly self-published, and the figures could not be tied to individual pages.
- No marketplace data was gathered (Fiverr/Upwork Italia, ProntoPro, Cronoshare for web developers).
