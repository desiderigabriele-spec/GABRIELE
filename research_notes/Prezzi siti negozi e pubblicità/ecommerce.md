# Prezzi di mercato e-commerce / negozi online (Shopify, WooCommerce): Milano e Italia, 2025-2026

> **Note on method (read first):** WebFetch could not reach any site in this session (DNS errors on every URL, shopify.com included). Every figure below comes from WebSearch result extracts of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Where a search summary mixed several sources in one sentence, I give the attribution I judge most likely and mark it "(attribution uncertain)". Almost all price sources are agencies or freelancers selling the same service, so they are marketing ranges, not neutral surveys. Unless a source says so, it is **not stated whether prices include IVA**. Italian B2B quotes are normally given "+ IVA 22%"; that is my inference, not something the sources state.
>
> **Studio prices being judged:** Start €3.900 / Growth €7.900 / Signature €14.000. Extras: +100 products €390, advanced email flows €690, connection to the management software (gestionale) €1.500, extra language €590.

## 1. Published price ranges for Shopify stores (theme setup, custom design, Plus): Milan vs rest of Italy, freelance vs agency

### Takeaway
Published Italian ranges sort into three bands. Freelancers charge about €500–3.000 (median €850–1.400). Agencies charge about €3.000–8.000 for a customised theme or template. Custom builds or Shopify Plus run €8.000–25.000+. Milan agencies that publish prices start at about €3.500 for a ~50-product store. The studio's Start (€3.900), Growth (€7.900) and Signature (€14.000) therefore sit in the low-middle, middle and middle-high of the **agency** market. They are 3–5x the typical freelance price.

### Inferences
- **Start €3.900** (customised theme, 50 products, payments, shipping, emails, analytics, Pixel):
  - About **€400 above** the most directly comparable published Milan offer (DSI Design from €3.500 for up to 50 products).
  - Inside Webcom's Milan "Professional" band (€3.500–8.000), above its base band (€1.000–3.500).
  - About 3–4x the freelance median (€850–1.400).
  - **Fair for a Milan studio. It needs clear value differentiators against freelancers at €800–2.500.**
- **Growth €7.900** (custom design, 300 products, copywriting, automated emails, reviews, SEO): at the top of the "Professional/template" bands (€3.500–8.000; Libellula €3.500–7.000 + IVA). It is inside DSI's €5.000–8.000 range for 50–200 products and below Gavriella Stella's €5.000–12.000 for up to 500 products with custom design. **In line with the market.** Truly custom design plus 300 products uploaded with sales copy is good value at this price.
- **Signature €14.000** (3D or configurator, ERP connection, top speed): at the low end of "Advanced/custom" (Webcom €8.000–20.000; Smart-Dato €8.000–25.000). 3D configurators alone are quoted at €5.000–20.000 (see section 2) and ERP integrations at €2.000–15.000. If both are real custom builds, **€14.000 is likely underpriced**. The math works only if the 3D part uses an app or a viewer, or the ERP part uses a ready connector.
- The Milan premium is not clearly measurable. Milan figures (DSI €3.500, Webcom €1.000–8.000, Gavriella Stella €5.000–12.000) look similar to national guides (Libellula €3.500–7.000, Smart-Dato €3.000–8.000). The most visible gap is freelance versus agency, not Milan versus the rest of Italy.

### Gaps
- No Milan agency publishes a Shopify Plus build price. The only figure found is Webcom's "Advanced/Plus €8.000–20.000", which is a generic band.
- IVA inclusion is stated only by Libellula ("IVA esclusa"). The other sources do not specify.
- No independent survey (Netcomm, Casaleggio, Osservatori) of average agency build prices was found.

## 2. Prices of extras: product upload, Klaviyo/email flows, ERP/gestionale integrations, multi-language, 3D viewers and configurators

### Takeaway
- **+100 products for €390** (€3,90/product) is mid-to-high for plain data entry but cheap for full listings with SEO.
- **Advanced email flows at €690** is low against Italian Klaviyo-agency setup fees (€300–3.000).
- **Gestionale connection at €1.500** fits app-based connectors (Fatture in Cloud, Danea) but is far below custom ERP integrations (€5.000–15.000+).
- **Extra language at €590** is near the bottom of the €500–2.000 setup range quoted for Shopify multi-language, and cheap if it includes translating the catalogue.

### Inferences
- **+100 products for €390 (€3,90 each):** above plain data entry (€0,30–1,50) and inside the "full" band (€2,50–10). Defensible if each product includes SEO text, images and variants. Expensive if it is a CSV import.
- **Advanced email flows for €690:** below Woow Labs' €1.500–3.000 setup and near the middle of its other €300–1.000 range. **Probably underpriced** if it includes design plus several segmented flows. A price around €900–1.500 would still be competitive (my inference).
- **Gestionale connection for €1.500:** realistic only with ready apps (MyAppify, MarketRock: free to low-cost licences) plus configuration. For custom TeamSystem or Zucchetti integrations the market quotes €10.000–15.000. The studio should state this extra covers "app-based connectors", and quote custom ERP work separately.
- **Signature's 3D or configurator:** a basic 3D viewer (models at €40–200 each plus an app at $25–39/month) fits the €14.000 package. A true custom configurator (€5.000–20.000 by itself) does not fit alongside ERP work and custom design.
- **Extra language for €590:** at the bottom of the €500–2.000 setup range. Translating the catalogue (€0,08–0,15/word) should be priced separately or capped.

- No Milan-specific product-upload price list was found.

## 3. Ongoing costs the client pays: Shopify plans in EUR, apps, payment fees

### Takeaway
Shopify Italy prices appear to have risen in 2025–2026: euro figures on third-party sites range from €19 to €36/month for Basic. The most recent detailed Italian guide (March 2026) gives Basic €29/month on annual billing (€36 monthly), Grow €82 (€105 monthly), Advanced €384, and Plus from about €2.100/month on a 3-year contract. Shopify Payments fees are quoted at either 1,9% + €0,25 (older figures) or 2,1% + €0,30 (2026 figures) on Basic. With apps (€50–200/month), clients should expect about €100–300/month in running costs before marketing. **The official Shopify Italy page could not be read in this session.**

### Inferences
- For a Start client on Basic, a realistic first-year running cost is about €290–430 for the plan, plus €600–2.400 for apps, plus Klaviyo €0–1.800, plus payment fees of about 2% + €0,25–0,30 per order. That totals roughly **€1.000–4.500/year** before marketing (my estimate from the figures above).
- Whether Shopify plan prices include IVA is unclear. EU B2B customers with a VAT number normally get reverse charge (inference, not verified). The studio's proposals should show running costs separately and "+ IVA".

## 4. Market averages and surveys (Casaleggio Associati, Netcomm) on SME e-commerce

### Takeaway
The Italian market is large and growing slowly (about 6%/year). There are **91.000** Italian companies with their own e-commerce site (+3,4% vs 2024). No Netcomm or Casaleggio figure on how much SMEs spend to *build* a store was found.

### Cited findings
- Netcomm (Netcomm Forum, April 2025):
  - Italian B2c online purchases in 2025 **exceed €62 billion (+6%)**; services €22 billion (+8%).
  - 35,2 million online shoppers (+1,5 million).
  - **91.000 Italian companies have their own e-commerce site (+3,4% vs 2024).**
  - — [Teleborsa – E-commerce B2c supererà i 62 miliardi nel 2025](https://www.teleborsa.it/News/2025/04/15/e-commerce-il-b2c-superera-i-62-miliardi-di-euro-nel-2025-6percent-e-in-italia-sono-35-2-milioni-i-consumatori-online-57.html); [Pagamenti Digitali – Report Netcomm](https://www.pagamentidigitali.it/ecommerce/ecommerce-b2c-in-italia-nel-2025-superati-i-62-miliardi-di-euro-6-cresce-il-numero-di-consumatori-digitali-e-aziende-con-sito-attivo/)
- Netcomm (May 2025):
  - Online product purchases in 2025 about **€40,1 billion (+6%)**.
  - Online share of product consumption **11,2%** (+0,5 points).
  - Food & grocery and beauty & pharma about +7%.
  - — [AGI – Acquisti online crescono 2025](https://www.agi.it/economia/news/2025-05-27/acquisti-online-crescono-miliardi-euro-2025-31592630/)
- Casaleggio Associati, "Ecommerce Italia 2025":
  - Italian e-commerce turnover in 2024 **€85,4 billion (+6%)**, with nearly a third of growth due to inflation.
  - Marketplace growth slowed from 55% (2023) to 1% (2024).
  - 64% of companies prioritised increasing turnover for 2025.
  - 45% worked on brand awareness (vs 34% in 2024).
  - — [Inside Marketing – Casaleggio Ecommerce Italia 2025](https://www.insidemarketing.it/casaleggio-associati-ecommerce-italia-2025/); [Casaleggio on X](https://x.com/casaleggio/status/1911813373592821979)
- Casaleggio Associati, "Ecommerce Italia 2026" (report #27, 1 April 2026):
  - 2025 turnover about **€90,6 billion (+6,1%)**, partly due to about 2% inflation.
  - Tourism over €22 billion, marketplaces €17,1 billion.
  - Mobile 42,1% of online transactions.
  - — [Casaleggio – Ecommerce Italia 2026 (PDF)](https://www.ecommerceitalia.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Ecommerce_Italia_2026-ITA-2.pdf)
  - The two Casaleggio reports disagree on the 2024 marketplace figure (€14,8 billion vs a recalculated value). Netcomm and Casaleggio measure different things (Netcomm: Italian consumers' B2c purchases; Casaleggio: e-commerce company turnover), so their totals are not comparable.

### Inferences
- About 91.000 companies with a site at +3,4%/year means roughly 3.000 new company e-commerce sites a year in Italy (my arithmetic). Many existing stores will also re-platform or redesign. Demand for small builds exists, but competition with freelancers and templates is strong.

### Gaps
- **No Netcomm or Casaleggio survey of average SME spend on building or running an e-commerce site was found.** The figures above describe market size, not project budgets.
- No Osservatori Digital Innovation (Politecnico di Milano) data on SME project spend was found in this session.
