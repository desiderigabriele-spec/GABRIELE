# Prezzi di mercato in Italia (2025-2026) per automazioni e integrazioni (n8n / Make / Zapier / codice) e automazione di processi con AI

Research date: 2026-10-20. Benchmarks the proposed levels: **"Automazione" 1.200 € una tantum** (2 programs connected, AI reads/sorts, alerts, testing, instructions, 1-2 weeks) + extras **900 € per extra program** and **49 €/mese monthly check**; **"AI su un processo" 4.900 € + 249 €/mese**.

**Method caveat (read first):** direct page fetching was blocked in this environment (WebFetch DNS failures and proxy 403 on curl for every Italian vendor domain tried). All figures below come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from reading each page in full. Where a search extract grouped several pages and the exact page behind a figure was unclear, this is flagged "(attribution uncertain)". Almost all Italian price data comes from vendors' own marketing blogs/pricing pages, not neutral surveys. Unless stated otherwise, Italian B2B prices are normally quoted **IVA esclusa** (IVA ordinaria 22%); explicit IVA statements are noted where the source made one.

## 1. What Italian agencies/freelancers charge per automation/workflow, per hour/day, and for retainers/maintenance

### Takeaway
Italian public price points for a single n8n/Make workflow cluster around **600-2.500 € one-off** (a published entry list price is 690 € per process + 89 €/month; several agencies quote "from 1.500 €"). Maintenance retainers range from roughly **89 €/month (small, includes server)** up to **1.200-2.000 €/month (enterprise/partner)**. The proposed 1.200 € is in the low-to-middle part of the market for one workflow with AI. The 49 €/month check is cheaper than the cheapest published Italian retainer found (89 €/month, which also includes hosting).

### Cited Findings
**Per-workflow / per-project prices (Italy)**

The fee covers the n8n server, error monitoring and changes when APIs change. — [andreabaglioni.it](https://andreabaglioni.it/blog/n8n-cos-e-come-funziona/)
- Tready (agency): **basic workflow 800-2.500 € one-off**. **Hourly / daily rates (Italy)**

- Generic Italian freelance guide: senior profile (5+ years) **60-100+ €/hour**. This covers all freelancers, not automation specifically. — [sevedemo.com](https://www.sevedemo.com/blog/quanto-costa-freelance-italia)
- Usanest: n8n freelance **50-90 €/hour** (see above). — [usanest.it](https://usanest.it/blog/consulenza-n8n-italia)

### Inferences
- **1.200 € "Automazione":** Italian single-workflow anchors are 690 € (Baglioni, plus a mandatory 89 €/month), 800-2.500 € (Tready), 600-3.000 € (Outline), 400-2.000 € medium complexity (Usanest), 900 € single use case (Cascades), and "from 1.500 €" (one agency). So 1.200 € with AI classification, alerts, testing and documentation is **competitive, around the market median**, and below agencies that start at 1.500 €. It is above the cheapest no-code offers (100-200 € setup).
- **900 € extra program:** no Italian source prices an "additional connected app" separately. Compared with per-workflow prices (690-1.500 € for a full process), 900 € for each extra connection is on the **high side** if it is only one more node or connector, and fair if it means a new integration with its own logic and testing. Consider tiering it (for example a lower price for a standard connector, 900 € for a custom API or a gestionale).
- **49 €/month check:** below Baglioni's 89 €/month (which includes the n8n server) and far below agency retainers (500-2.000 €/month). Outline's 15-20%/year rule applied to 1.200 € gives about 15-20 €/month, so 49 €/month (588 €/year, about 49% of the build price) is **above the "percentage" rule but below every published Italian retainer**. It is defensible if it includes monitoring and small fixes. State clearly whether hosting/platform fees are included (Baglioni's 89 € includes the server).
- Implied rate: 1.200 € for 1-2 weeks of calendar time is only realistic if billable effort is about 12-20 hours (60-100 €/hour Italian freelance band). Inference only.

### Gaps
- Could not open the vendor pages directly (network block), so the scope behind each price (number of steps, AI included or not) could not be checked line by line.
- No neutral Italian survey of automation project prices was found. All data are vendor self-reports.
- No Italian source found that prices "extra connected program" as a separate item.

## 2. Freelance rates for automation specialists (Malt, Upwork, Fiverr, n8n/Make directories) – Italy and Europe

### Takeaway
No Italy-filtered marketplace rate data was found. ### Inferences
- European professional day rates (350-660 €/day) mean 1-2 weeks of full-time work would cost 1.750-6.600 €. The proposed 1.200 € therefore assumes reusable templates and/or only part-time effort over the 1-2 weeks. This supports 1.200 € as a **value price, not a premium price**.
- Offshore Upwork prices ($17-45/hour) are a low-cost substitute that Italian SMEs can reach. The Italian-language service, local support and GDPR handling are the differentiators.

### Gaps
- No Malt Italy (malt.it) rate data for n8n/Make was found. The official Malt Freelance Tech Barometer for Italy was not reached.
- Official n8n Experts directory and Make Partner directory pages show no prices (not retrieved).
- Fiverr gig-level data for Italy was not found.

### Inferences
- **4.900 € setup:** inside 1.500-5.000 € (pilot band, at the top), 3.000-8.000 € (mid), 2.000-8.000 € (SNJ), and at the bottom of Rayo's 4.000-12.000 € for document AI integrated with a gestionale. The price is credible for one end-to-end process with human approval and a dashboard. It is lower than typical quotes when deep ERP integration (Zucchetti/TeamSystem) is required.
- **249 €/month:** inside 200-600 € (pilot), 100-300 €, and 200-800 € (SNJ), and below 300-800 € (Rayo document AI). It is well above the actual running costs (see section 4: typically €20-60/month of platform + a few €/month to tens of €/month of LLM for SME volumes). The fee is therefore mostly margin for monitoring, support and improvement, which matches how vendors describe their fees (they cover AI tokens, hosting and maintenance of integrations).
- Competitive pressure: SaaS AI agents such as Agensio (82,50-450 €/month with no setup fee) compete with the email-triage use case. A custom 4.900 € offer needs to stress fit to the client's processes, data ownership and integration.

### Gaps
- No public Italian price list for "preventivi automatici" (automatic quote generation).
- Vendor sources do not separate setup effort by sub-feature (OCR vs. approval UI vs. dashboard).
- No Italian price data on the volumes behind the monthly fees (how many documents or emails are included).

### Takeaway
Platform fees for an SME are small. n8n Cloud costs **€20-50/month** (or €5-20/month for a self-hosted VPS), Make about **$9-16/month**, and Zapier **$19.99-49+/month**. LLM API costs for typical SME volumes (hundreds to a few thousand emails or documents per month) are usually **single-digit to low tens of €/month**. Official vendor pages could not be reached, so all figures are third-party. Verify them before quoting.

### Cited Findings
**n8n (third-party summaries of official pricing; prices in EUR, plus IVA)**

### Inferences (ESTIMATES, own calculation from the per-token prices above)
- Email triage, 1.000 emails/month, about 1.500 input + 300 output tokens each = 1,5M in + 0,3M out. Cost: about **$3/month on Haiku 4.5**, about **$9/month on Sonnet 4.6**.
- Invoice/DDT reading, 500 documents/month, about 3.000 input + 500 output tokens each = 1,5M in + 0,25M out. Cost: about **$8/month on Sonnet 4.6**, about **$3/month on Haiku 4.5**. Scanned PDFs or images can use more tokens per page, so actual costs may be 2-3x higher. Estimate only.
- Total client running costs for the "Automazione" level are therefore about **€20-60/month** (n8n Cloud Starter/Pro or Make/Zapier entry plan + a few € of LLM). For the "AI su un processo" level they are about **€30-100/month**. The proposed 49 €/month and 249 €/month fees should state whether these costs are included or billed separately.
- Official prices are in USD for Make, Zapier, Anthropic and OpenAI. Italian clients pay them by card, often with reverse-charge/IVA handling on foreign services. Exchange-rate exposure should be noted if the fee includes them.

### Gaps
- Official pricing pages (n8n.io/pricing, make.com/pricing, zapier.com/pricing, anthropic.com/pricing, openai.com/api/pricing) could not be fetched directly. Figures are from aggregators and contain conflicts (Make Core $9 vs $12; Haiku current model and price).

## 5. Italian surveys on SME AI adoption and spend, and grants affecting pricing (Voucher Doppia Transizione, Transizione 5.0 / iperammortamento)

### Takeaway
Italian SMEs mostly still don't invest in AI: **76% neither invest nor plan to** (Polimi, May 2026). Grants change the price conversation. The **Voucher Doppia Transizione 2026** covers **50% (up to 70% in some chambers) up to €10.000**, but in Lombardia it requires **at least €4.000 of eligible spend**, **qualified suppliers**, and a consulting share of 30-70%. The **Transizione 5.0 tax credit ended on 31/12/2025** and was replaced for 2026-Sept 2028 by **iperammortamento**, which covers AI software (Allegato V). A 4.900 € project clears the Lombardia minimum; a standalone 1.200 € automation does not.

### Cited Findings
**Adoption / spend surveys**
- Osservatorio Innovazione Digitale nelle PMI (Politecnico di Milano), data released **21 May 2026**: **76% of Italian SMEs have not invested and do not plan to invest in AI**. **Voucher Doppia Transizione 2026**
- National scheme by Unioncamere through the Camere di commercio PID: grant under de minimis, about **€150M for 2026-2029**. Typically **50% of eligible spend, up to 70% for micro/small firms in some chambers, cap €10.000**. AI is eligible (in Lombardia: ML, deep learning, NLP, **LLM, AI agents**, data mining). AI consulting must relate to **a specific business use case with concrete, documentable outputs**. Services must come from the **qualified suppliers** defined by the call. — [agendadigitale.eu](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/industry-4-0/voucher-doppia-transizione-2026-la-guida-per-le-pmi/); [pmi.it](https://www.pmi.it/economia/finanziamenti/496943/voucher-doppia-transizione-pmi.html); [assolombarda.it](https://www.assolombarda.it/servizi/credito-finanza-e-incentivi/informazioni/bando-voucher-doppia-transizione-2026-contributi-per-digitalizzazione-e-sostenibilita); [infominds.eu](https://infominds.eu/bandi-digitalizzazione/)
- **Lombardia 2026:** 50% grant, **max €10.000** (+ up to €500 premium for legality rating or gender-equality certification). **Minimum eligible investment €4.000.** Budget **€4.463.500** (of which €460.000 for Cremona-Mantova-Pavia). Applications prepared from 8 July 2026 and submitted **from 11:00 on 28 July 2026** via the ReStart (InfoCamere) platform, first come first served. Firms that benefited from the 2025 call are excluded. In Lombardia, consulting/training must be **30-70% of total project spend**. Expenses must be incurred and reported **by 30 April 2027**. — [industrialombarda.it](https://industrialombarda.it/2026/07/22/voucher-doppia-transizione-lombardia-2026/); [cnalombardianordovest.it](https://www.cnalombardianordovest.it/agevolazioni/voucher-doppia-transizione-lombardia-2026/); [confartigianatopavia.com](https://www.confartigianatopavia.com/voucher-doppia-transizione-2026-contributi-per-le-pmi-lombarde); [agendadigitale.eu](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/industry-4-0/voucher-doppia-transizione-2026-la-guida-per-le-pmi/)

**Transizione 5.0 → iperammortamento 2026-2028**
- The Transizione 5.0 credit applies only to investments completed by **31 Dec 2025**. From 2026 the Legge di Bilancio 2026 (**L. 199/2025**) replaced it with **iperammortamento**. **AI software is in Allegato V**. One source cites a **180% increase in deductible cost up to €2,5M** of investment. Investments from **1 Jan 2026 to 30 Sept 2028** are covered. Interconnection matters, and energy saving is no longer required. — [incentivimpresa.it](https://www.incentivimpresa.it/transizione-5-0-come-accedere-agli-incentivi-per-la-doppia-transizione/); [incentivimpresa.it – software AI](https://www.incentivimpresa.it/transizione-5-0-software-ai-spese-ammissibili-2026/); [quadrasrl.net](https://quadrasrl.net/iperammortamento-2026-2028-ex-transizione-5-0-guida/)
- **Cloud/SaaS eligibility is disputed.** One source says it is excluded (about 80% of the Italian enterprise software market, per Anitec-Assinform). Another says it can qualify if it fits Allegato V and the technical requirements. — [lucasammarco.com](https://lucasammarco.com/blog/transizione-5-0-2026-2028-ai-pmi); [pmi.it](https://www.pmi.it/impresa/normativa/484923/iperammortamento-2026-decreto-attuativo-regole-novita.html)
- The implementing decree was signed by MIMIT on 4 May 2026 and by MEF on 7 May 2026, and the GSE booking platform opened on **12 June 2026**. Another source said the decree was still awaiting Corte dei Conti registration (status at 20 Oct 2026 unconfirmed). — [pmi.it](https://www.pmi.it/impresa/normativa/484923/iperammortamento-2026-decreto-attuativo-regole-novita.html); [tentamus.it](https://www.tentamus.it/news/piano-transizione-5-0-iperammortamento-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Grant impact on pricing:** with the Lombardia voucher, a 4.900 € project costs the client a net **about 2.450 €** at 50% if the supplier is qualified and the project meets the consulting-share and documentation rules. Bundling the 1.200 € automation with extras or training (for example 1.200 + 900 + training ≥ 4.000 €) would be needed to reach the €4.000 minimum. Being (or partnering with) a **qualified supplier** under the voucher is a commercial advantage.
- The very low SME AI adoption (76% not investing) suggests a price-sensitive, early market. Entry-level offers around 1.000-1.500 € with low monthly fees fit that profile.
- Iperammortamento mainly benefits profitable companies buying owned or licensed AI software (deduction, not a cash grant). It is less relevant to small services projects, and recurring SaaS-style fees may not qualify.
- **IVA:** Italian B2B quotes should state "+ IVA 22%". A freelancer in the regime forfettario does not charge IVA (general Italian tax rule, not sourced here), which makes forfettario freelancers about 22% cheaper for clients that can't recover IVA. For B2B clients IVA is normally recoverable.
