# Ready-made management software (gestionali pronti / SaaS) in Italy: real 2025-2026 prices vs a custom gestionale

Date checked: 2026-10-20. Method note: WebFetch and direct HTTPS to vendor sites (fattureincloud.it, odoo.com, danea.it, aliteq.com) were blocked from this environment (DNS / proxy 403). Every figure below comes from WebSearch results that quote the official pages or Italian review/partner sites. Prices are IVA esclusa unless noted otherwise. Treat each figure as "reported by source X". Re-check the official page before showing a figure to a client. Calculated totals are marked **[calc]**. Estimates are marked **[estimate]**.

## Public list prices of the main ready-made products

### Takeaway
Invoicing-type tools (Fatture in Cloud, Aruba, Danea Easyfatt) cost tens to a few hundred euro a year. Real ERPs (Odoo, TeamSystem, Zucchetti, Passepartout) start at about 1.000-5.000 €/year once users and modules are added. Odoo is the only real ERP with a transparent public per-user price. TeamSystem Lynfa/Gamma, Zucchetti Ad Hoc and Mago4, and Passepartout Mexal publish no list price ("su preventivo", depends on modules and users).

### Inferences
- Odoo's Custom plan of 35,90/44,90 € is about 20% above the earlier 29,90/37,40 € (37,40 × 1,2 ≈ 44,88 €). This matches the reported Custom increase of September 2026. A client who needs customisation in Odoo (Studio, custom modules) has to be on Custom, the plan that went up.
- Fatture in Cloud, Aruba and Easyfatt are invoicing tools, not ERPs. Their low price matches a narrower scope (fatturazione elettronica, preventivi, DDT, scadenze). They do not cover workflow, production, or custom processes. For a custom gestionale, the fair comparison is Odoo, TeamSystem, Zucchetti or Passepartout plus partner services.
- First-year discounts are common: Odoo 19,90 → 24,90 €, Fatture in Cloud Forfettari 4 → 8 €, Aruba 1 € for 3 months. Year 1 understates the recurring cost.

### Gaps
- I could not open the official pricing pages (network blocked), so official figures for Fatture in Cloud (Premium and Premium Plus conflict), Danea (exact current euro amounts), and Cassa in Cloud tiers are unconfirmed.
- No public prices exist for TeamSystem Lynfa, Gamma, Alyante or Enterprise, Zucchetti Ad Hoc Revolution or Mago4, or Passepartout Mexal (base). These are quote-only through resellers.
- I found no official Fatture in Cloud price per extra user.

## Typical implementation and customisation costs from Italian partners and resellers

### Takeaway
Only Odoo partners publish indicative figures: 500-900 €/day (80-150 €/hour) for consultancy, 5.000-12.000 € for a basic project under 5 users, 8.000-30.000 € on average, and 35.000-80.000 € for 30+ users. Partners also suggest a yearly support fee of 15-20% of the implementation cost. The other vendors' resellers charge installation, start-up and training separately, by quote.

### Cited Findings
- Odoo consultant day rate: about 500-900 €/day depending on seniority. Hourly rate for certified partners: 80-150 €/hour — [gb-technology.com](https://gb-technology.com/blog/notizie-su-erp-e-mondo-odoo-2/implementazione-odoo-erp-tempi-costi-reali-e-cosa-aspettarsi-55); [maiamanagement.it](https://maiamanagement.it/odoo/quanto-costa-implementare-odoo-in-italia/)
- Functional Consultant rates by Odoo partner level: 450-650 €/day (Ready), 650-850 €/day (Silver), 850-1.100 €/day (Gold) — [brentasoft.com](https://brentasoft.com/blog/scegliere-partner-odoo-19-pmi-italiane-guida/)
- Odoo project cost: basic project under 5 users 5.000-12.000 €; complex project over 30 users 35.000-80.000 € — [gb-technology.com 2026](https://gb-technology.com/en/blog/erp-and-odoo-news-2/implementazione-odoo-erp-tempi-e-costi-reali-guida-2026-59). Brentasoft gives an average of 8.000-30.000 € — [brentasoft.com](https://brentasoft.com/soluzioni/odoo-erp.php). For an SME with 30-50 employees, 10.000-25.000 € of consultancy on top of licences — [maiamanagement.it](https://maiamanagement.it/odoo/quanto-costa-implementare-odoo-in-italia/). For a manufacturing SME, 15.000-70.000 € in the first year — [maiamanagement.it](https://maiamanagement.it/odoo/odoo-gestionale-cosa-sapere-prima-dellacquisto/)
- After go-live: budget a support fee of 15-20%/year of the implementation cost — [gb-technology.com](https://gb-technology.com/blog/notizie-su-erp-e-mondo-odoo-2/implementazione-odoo-erp-tempi-costi-reali-e-cosa-aspettarsi-55)
- HubSpot: mandatory onboarding of 1.470 € (Sales Pro) to 3.420 € (Enterprise) — [blog.hubspot.de](https://blog.hubspot.de/sales/hubspot-sales-hub-pricing)
- Passepartout: app prices exclude "installazione, avviamento e assistenza a cura del distributore" — [passstore.passepartout.net](https://passstore.passepartout.net/Apps/Detail/ricalcolo-prezzi-listino/667)
- Caveat: every implementation figure comes from partners who sell these services (a commercial interest). No official Odoo source for partner rates exists.

### Inferences
- **[estimate]** If TeamSystem and Zucchetti resellers charge day rates like Odoo partners (500-900 €/day), a 5-10 day start-up (installation, data import, training, light customisation) would add about 2.500-9.000 € in year 1. I found no TeamSystem or Zucchetti reseller day rate to confirm this.
- Customisation on a packaged ERP is paid again at every major upgrade, because custom modules have to be migrated. Partner guides hint at this through the 15-20% yearly support fee.

### Gaps
- No published day rate for TeamSystem, Zucchetti or Passepartout resellers.
- No public training-package prices for Fatture in Cloud, Danea or Aruba (these are mostly self-service).

## Hidden costs: per-user fees, modules, annual price increases, data lock-in

### Takeaway
The recurring cost of off-the-shelf software grows in four ways. Per-user fees (Odoo, CRMs, Aruba). Paid modules (Aruba goes from 29,90 to 134,60 €/year; Fatture in Cloud needs Premium Plus or above for multi-user). First-year promos that end. Vendor price changes (Odoo Custom about +20% in 2026; some Italian vendors adjust fees yearly to ISTAT and contractually exclude the right to withdraw).

### Cited Findings
- Aruba: the base costs 29,90 €/year, but preventivi and incassi are extra modules, so the cart renewal reaches 134,60 €/year. Each extra user costs 4,90 €/year — [profattura.it](https://www.profattura.it/guide/aruba-fatturazione-elettronica-quando-cambiare); [quasa.io](https://quasa.io/it/media/fatture-in-cloud-o-aruba-il-prezzo-basso-si-ferma-ai-moduli-necessari)
- Fatture in Cloud: base plans are single-user, adding collaborators costs more, and multi-user starts with Premium Plus — [srlonline.com](https://www.srlonline.com/software-gestionali-2026-fatture-in-cloud-vs-danea-teamsystem-confronto-prezzi-funzioni/)
- Odoo: the first-year discount ends (19,90 → 24,90 € Standard; 35,90 → 44,90 € Custom). Monthly billing costs about 25% more — [aliteq.com](https://aliteq.com/odoo-pricing-community-vs-enterprise). Custom rose by about 20% in Sept 2026 (unverified) — [LinkedIn](https://dk.linkedin.com/in/alexlyngsoe); [Brussels Times](https://www.brusselstimes.com/business/2332306/odoo-announces-e10-billion-valuation-and-a-partial-price-increase)
- Odoo: customisation (Studio, custom modules, external API) is only available on the more expensive Custom plan — [aliteq.com](https://aliteq.com/odoo-pricing-community-vs-enterprise)
- Danea multi-postazione: one reseller says the yearly renewal is mandatory from the second year (396 € + IVA/year) — [omnitekstore.it](https://www.omnitekstore.it/easyfatt-invio-telematico/1849-danea-easyfatt-enterprise-software-gestionale-9682201379454.html)
- Zucchetti connector: e-commerce integration is an extra fee (1.690 € the first year, 890 €/year after) — [creawebonline.it](https://www.creawebonline.it/presta/gestionali/22-sincronizzazione-zucchetti-adhoc-revolution-web-con-e-commerce-prestashop.html)
- ISTAT indexation clause (example from an Italian software vendor, ICCOM, not TeamSystem or Zucchetti): fees are adjusted yearly to ISTAT indices with 30 days' notice, and the adjustment "non dà diritto a recedere senza costi" — [iccom.it](https://www.iccom.it/wp-content/uploads/ICCOM-Comunicazione-Aumento-Prezzi-15-1-24.pdf)
- TeamSystem: conservazione sostitutiva and extra modules push the total above 3.000-4.000 €/year (competitor source) — [bomasoftware.it](https://www.bomasoftware.it/alternativa-teamsystem/)
- HubSpot becomes expensive as a company grows. Pipedrive and Zoho scale more predictably — [sales-surge.io](https://www.sales-surge.io/resources/crm-comparison/)

### Inferences
- Lock-in: with SaaS, a client who stops paying loses access to the software. Data can usually be exported (CSV/XML), but configured workflows and customisations cannot be moved. This is general industry knowledge. I found no Italian source documenting a specific export restriction, so present it as a structural risk, not a documented abuse.

### Gaps
- I found no TeamSystem or Zucchetti contract clauses on ISTAT adjustments, and no documented size of their yearly increases.
- I found no documented case of an Italian SaaS gestionale refusing or charging for data export.

## Example 3-year and 5-year TCO for 5 and 15 users vs the custom offers

### Takeaway
At 5 users, invoicing SaaS (a few hundred € to about 3.000 € over 5 years) is far cheaper than any custom option, but it covers much less. A real ERP with partner setup (Odoo Standard plus implementation) costs about 9.000-19.000 € over 5 years, the same range as the custom 4.900 € and 8.900 € offers. At 15 users, Odoo with implementation (about 30.000-52.000 € over 5 years on Standard) or HubSpot Pro (about 82.000 €) cost more than even the 13.900 € custom offer (28.840 € over 5 years), because the custom fee does not grow per user.
