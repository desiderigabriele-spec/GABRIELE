# Prezzi di mercato in Italia (2025-2026) per gestionali su misura per PMI

> **How this was collected.** Direct page fetches (WebFetch) failed with DNS errors for every Italian domain tried (lemio.it, codebaker.it, regispro.it, craon.it, talentup.io, pasqualepillitteri.it), and the shell proxy check was blocked. So every figure below comes from **search-engine summaries of the cited pages** (WebSearch, "extended" mode), not from reading the pages in full. The numbers are specific and the URLs are real, but the report writer should treat each one as "reported by the page per search summary". Spot-check the key pages before publishing.
>
> **Source bias.** Almost every price source is the blog of an Italian software house, consultant or partner that sells the service. None is an independent survey. Wecoode itself says no independent Italian price survey for custom gestionali exists (see Q1). Prices are 2026 figures unless noted.
>
> **Proposed price list being checked (Studio):** Essenziale 4.900 € + 79 €/mese (1 area, ≤5 users, Excel import, 4-6 weeks). Completo 8.900 € + 149 €/mese (≤4 connected areas, roles, dashboard, e-invoicing, 8-10 weeks). Completo + AI 13.900 € + 249 €/mese (AI drafting, email/invoice reading, chat Q&A, 10-12 weeks). Extras: area 1.500 €, AI function 1.900 €, data migration 900 €, phone app 1.200 €.

## 1. Published price ranges for a custom web gestionale (small / medium / complex)

### Takeaway
Italian vendors publish a consistent range. A small gestionale (1-3 modules, MVP) costs **about 3.500-15.000 €**, and the most common starting point is 5.000-10.000 €. A complete multi-module PMI gestionale costs **about 8.000-35.000 €** (some vendors say 25.000-60.000 €). Complex or ERP-like projects start at **25.000-50.000 €**. Essenziale at 4.900 € sits at the low end of the "small" band. Completo at 8.900 € sits at or below the bottom of the "complete PMI gestionale" band.

### Cited Findings
**Software-house price guides (2026):**
- LEMIO gives three tiers. An MVP costs 3.500-8.000 € and takes 4-8 weeks (master data, 2-3 core processes, basic dashboard). A complete PMI gestionale costs 8.000-25.000 € and takes 3-6 months. A custom ERP or SaaS platform costs 25.000-80.000 €+. — [LEMIO, "Quanto costa un gestionale aziendale? 3.500-25.000 € (2026)"](https://lemio.it/blog/quanto-costa-gestionale-aziendale-2026)

RegisPro has a bias, since it sells a "modular alternative". — [RegisPro, Quanto costa un gestionale su misura 2026](https://www.regispro.it/blog/quanto-costa-gestionale-su-misura-2026)

### Inferences
- **The market has three layers:** (a) freelancers and micro-studios at 1.500-5.000 € (Piani, La Capitale, CRAON, ProntoPro); (b) small software houses at 5.000-15.000 € for a first version (LEMIO, RegisPro, Nesso, Codebaker, Nebu); (c) structured software houses at 15.000-60.000 €+ (One AM, Matech, SPS, Codebaker medium).
- **Essenziale (4.900 €, 4-6 weeks)** is at the bottom of layer (b) and close to LEMIO's MVP band (3.500-8.000 €, 4-8 weeks). It is credible, slightly cheap for a software house, and above the freelancer layer. Raising it to 5.500-6.500 € would still sit below Nesso Light's midpoint and Nebu's 10.000 € start.
- **Completo (8.900 €, ≤4 areas, roles, dashboard, e-invoicing, 8-10 weeks)** is **underpriced against the market**. Comparable scope is priced at 8.000-25.000 € (LEMIO, 3-6 months), 12.000-35.000 € (Nesso middle band), 15.000-50.000 € (Codebaker medium) and 25.000-60.000 € (Matech). Nebu charges 10.000 € for the first module alone. A price of **12.000-16.000 €** would still be at or below the median of published bands. The 8-10 week delivery is fast compared with LEMIO's 3-6 months and Nesso's 16-30 weeks for multi-module work.
- **Completo + AI (13.900 €)**: see Q5. The AI premium (+5.000 €) is low against AI integration pricing.
- **State IVA esclusa explicitly** ("+ IVA"), as Nebu and Matech do. This is my inference about common B2B practice, not a sourced rule.

### Gaps
- No independent or statistical survey (Osservatori PoliMi, Il Sole 24 Ore, Agenda Digitale) giving euro price ranges for custom PMI gestionali was found. Agenda Digitale results were only vendor white papers.
- Preventivi.it, Instapro and Habitissimo price pages for "gestionale" were not found. ProntoPro was the only marketplace figure.
- No real published quotes (preventivi with line items) were found, only vendor guides.
- No Fiverr or Upwork data for Italian freelancers' gestionale gigs was found.

## 2. Italian developer day rates and hourly rates (freelance and agency), with cost estimates for 20/40/80 dev-days

### Takeaway
Italian freelance developers charge roughly **230-450 €/day** (mid-level) and **440-700 €/day** (senior). Software houses and Odoo partners charge **450-1.200 €/day** (about 60-150 €/hour). Assintel's 2025 average for IT professionals is reported as **347 €/day (programmer) to 540 €/day (consultant)**.

### Cited Findings
- Assintel Report 2025 (as cited by secondary sources): average daily rates for IT professionals in Italy run from **347 € (programmatore) to 540 € (consulente)**. The secondary source says this is not an official price list. The per-profile tables in the report itself could not be verified. — [Pasquale Pillitteri, Tariffe freelance ICT Italia 2026](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/453/tariffe-freelance-ict-italia); report PDF: [Assintel Report 2025](https://youmark-images.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Assintel-Report-2025.pdf); context: [Assintel press release, 03/11/2025](https://www.assintel.it/sala-stampa-2/assintel-report-2025-il-digitale-italiano-vale-oltre-44-miliardi-cresce-del-45-e-guarda-al-futuro-con-agenda-in-10-punti/)

### Inferences (estimates, not sourced figures)
Cost of dev-days at three representative rates: 300 €/day (mid freelance or small studio), 450 €/day (senior freelance, LEMIO's evolutive rate, Assintel range), 700 €/day (software house or Odoo partner):

| Dev-days | @300 €/day | @450 €/day | @700 €/day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 6.000 € | 9.000 € | 14.000 € |
| 40 | 12.000 € | 18.000 € | 28.000 € |
| 80 | 24.000 € | 36.000 € | 56.000 € |

- **Effort the proposed prices imply.** At 450 €/day: Essenziale (4.900 €) buys about 11 days; Completo (8.900 €) about 20 days; Completo + AI (13.900 €) about 31 days. At 300 €/day the figures are about 16, 30 and 46 days.
- **Can the timelines be staffed at those prices?** A 4-6 week Essenziale with one developer is 20-30 working days of calendar time. A 4-4 Completo over 8-10 weeks is 40-50 calendar days. Unless the studio reuses a base product or template, or uses heavy AI-assisted development, **revenue per actual dev-day falls toward 200-250 €**. That is at or below the LiberiPro mid-freelance level and well below software-house rates. The pricing therefore only works if a large share of each project is reused.
- **Extras.** "Area in più" at 1.500 € is about 3-5 dev-days, which is plausible for a CRUD area plus its links. The other extras are covered in Q5.

### Gaps
- Malt's official Italy freelance rates (barometer) were not found.
- Codemotion, Talent.io and TalentUp contractor reports for Italy: the TalentUp page exists ([TalentUp Italy contractor benchmark 2026](https://talentup.io/insights/it/contractors-benchmark-report/italy/)), but its developer figures could not be retrieved.
- The search summaries did not say whether the freelance rates quoted are IVA esclusa. For Italian freelancers on the regime forfettario, IVA does not apply.

## 3. Maintenance and support fees (canone) and hosting costs

### Takeaway
The market norm is **annual maintenance of 10-20% of the development cost**. Hosting and backup cost **500-1.500 €/year**, sometimes billed separately. A software house's entry canone can start at **950 €/year with hosting included** (Nebu), while enterprise-style SLA packages run 600-1.500 €/month. The proposed canoni equal about 19-21% of the development price per year, which is at the upper edge of the norm. They become competitive if they include hosting and normative updates.

### Cited Findings
- LEMIO lists recurring costs separately: corrective maintenance at **15-20% of the development value**; hosting and backup **500-1.500 €/year**; regulatory updates such as electronic invoicing **300-1.000 €/year**; evolutive work at 400-600 €/day. — [LEMIO](https://lemio.it/blog/quanto-costa-gestionale-aziendale-2026)
- RegisPro puts maintenance at 10-20% per year, plus hosting and support. Its 3-year total cost can be 40-60% above the initial price. — [RegisPro](https://www.regispro.it/blog/quanto-costa-gestionale-su-misura-2026)
- Nebu includes the first year of maintenance in the development price. **From year 2 the canone starts at 950 €/year**, covering European hosting, backup, technical and security updates and monitoring. — [Nebu](https://www.nebu.it/prezzi-gestionali)
- Another vendor's base maintenance package (small PMI, standard SLA) costs **600-1.500 €/month**. Attribution needs verifying; it is either [TN Solutions](https://www.tnsolutions.it/it/sviluppo-gestionale-su-misura-quanto-costa/) or [Noonic](https://noonic.com/risorse/guide/quanto-costa-software-gestionale-custom-2026).
- Codebaker and RegisPro: no per-user fees on custom software. — [Codebaker](https://www.codebaker.it/gestionale-personalizzato-manifatturiero); 15-20% of the initial cost per year, with no per-user fees, is also reported by [Sviluppatore Migliore](https://sviluppatoremigliore.com/blog/software-gestione-commesse)
- PWA or web app maintenance: about 10-15% of development per year, or a monthly fee of **150-500 €** including hosting and maintenance. — [SystemForge, PWA vs app nativa](https://systemforge.it/blog/pwa-vs-app-nativo-quale-scegliere/)
- SaaS benchmark: a full-user cloud gestionale often costs 25-60 €/user/month. Custom software reaches break-even against SaaS between month 18 and month 30. These figures are from search summaries of [FTV Tech](https://ftvtech.net/guide/costo-software-gestionale-pmi/) and [Sviluppatore Migliore](https://sviluppatoremigliore.com/blog/software-gestione-commesse), and the exact attribution is unclear. SaaS spend of 40-120 €/user/month, with break-even against custom at about 30.000 €/year of total spend, is from [Sviluppatore Migliore](https://sviluppatoremigliore.com/blog/software-gestione-commesse).

### Inferences
- **The proposed canoni as a share of development price:** 79 €/month is 948 €/year, or 19.3% of 4.900 €. 149 €/month is 1.788 €/year, or 20.1% of 8.900 €. 249 €/month is 2.988 €/year, or 21.5% of 13.900 €. All three sit at the **top of the 10-20% norm** relative to the (low) development prices. They are reasonable in absolute terms if they include hosting, backup, monitoring, regulatory updates and small fixes.
- 79 €/month (948 €/year) is almost exactly Nebu's 950 €/year entry canone, which includes hosting. It is credible and defensible for Essenziale.
- If Completo's dev price rises to 12.000-16.000 €, a 149 €/month canone falls to about 11-15%. That is mid-norm and an easier sell.
- Against SaaS, 5 users × 40 €/month is 200 €/month. Essenziale at 79 €/month undercuts that, which is a strong "tuo, non in affitto" argument. Completo at 149 €/month is equivalent to 3-4 SaaS seats.
- **Spell out what the canone includes**: hosting, backups, security updates, e-invoicing and regulatory updates, and a number of support hours. LEMIO bills these items separately.

### Gaps
- No standalone hosting price list for small custom web gestionali was found (e.g. VPS or managed hosting in euros per month). The only hosting figure is LEMIO's 500-1.500 €/year bundle.
- No data on SLA response times bundled with entry-level canoni was found.

## 4. Low-code and off-the-shelf alternatives (Odoo partners, AppSheet, Airtable, Softr) and their cost for PMI

### Takeaway
Implementing Odoo through an Italian partner typically costs **2.500-5.000 € (micro-firms)** and **8.000-35.000 € (PMI, first year)**. Licences are on top, at about **20-37 €/user/month**. No-code tools are cheap per seat (AppSheet 5-20 $, Airtable 20-54 $, Softr 19-119 $/month). No Italian consultant price data was found for those tools.

### Cited Findings
- Odoo implementation through Italian partners:
  - Andrea Piani: "Starter" package for micro-firms 2.500-5.000 €; "PMI Standard" 8.000-20.000 €. — [andreapiani.com, Servizi Odoo](https://www.andreapiani.com/servizi-odoo.html)
  - Maia Management: PMI with 10-15 users, 8.000-15.000 € for a standard implementation in 4-8 weeks. PMI with 10-25 employees (CRM, sales, purchasing, invoicing, warehouse), 15.000-35.000 € in the first year. Offers prepaid 50-hour packages. — [Maia Management](https://maiamanagement.it/odoo/quanto-costa-implementare-odoo-in-italia/)
  - GB Technology: intermediate project (4-8 modules plus some custom development) 15.000-40.000 €; day rates mostly 500-900 €. — [GB Technology](https://gb-technology.com/en/blog/erp-and-odoo-news-2/implementazione-odoo-erp-tempi-e-costi-reali-guida-2026-59)
  - XPMI: ongoing work with prepaid hour packages. — [XPMI](https://www.xpmi.it/servizi)
- Odoo licences in Italy (updated July 2026): **Standard 19,90 €/user/month** (annual, promotional) or 24,90 € list price; **Custom 29,90 €/user/month** (annual, promotional) or 37,40 € list price. Monthly billing costs more. — [oec.sh, Odoo pricing Italy](https://oec.sh/odoo-pricing/italy). Check against odoo.com/pricing.
- No-code tool prices, in USD; the sources disagree:
  - Softr: Basic at 19, 25 or 59 $/month depending on source and date; Pro at 119 $/month (API, CSV export, custom code). One analysis puts an internal app for 200 users at about 1.270 $/month. — [buildfastwithai, Softr review 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/ai-tools/softr); [Collabportals](https://www.collabportals.com/blog/alternatives-to-softr)
  - Airtable: Team about 20-24 $/user/month; Business about 45-54 $/user/month. — [Zite](https://www.zite.com/blog/no-code-app-builder-with-database)
  - AppSheet: Starter 5 $, Core 10 $, Enterprise Plus 20 $/user/month. — [Capterra, AppSheet](https://www.capterra.com/p/149367/AppSheet/)

### Inferences
- For 5 users, Odoo Custom costs about 150-190 €/month in licences alone, plus a partner implementation of 5.000-15.000 € for a small scope. Studio's **Completo at 8.900 € + 149 €/month with no per-user fee** is clearly cheaper over 3 years than an Odoo partner project of similar scope. That supports raising the Completo price.
- Odoo's micro-firm "Starter" (2.500-5.000 €) competes directly with Essenziale (4.900 €). Studio's differentiator is a fully tailored product with no licences.
- A DIY no-code route (Airtable plus Softr for 5 users) costs about 100-300 $/month in tools, but it needs the client's own time or a consultant.

### Gaps
- No Italian freelancer or agency prices for Airtable, Softr, AppSheet or Glide implementations were found.
- No Odoo partner price page combining partner tier with a fixed package price was retrieved directly.

## 5. Price ranges for AI features and mobile app/PWA add-ons

### Takeaway
Italian consultants price a **single AI use case (e.g. invoice reading linked to a gestionale) at about 4.000-10.000 € setup plus 150-400 €/month**. Custom AI on an existing gestionale costs 5.000-25.000 € plus 200-1.500 €/month. A **standalone PWA costs 6.000-40.000 €**. Studio's AI tier (+5.000 € for three AI functions), its 1.900 € AI extra and its 1.200 € phone app all sit **well below** these figures. They only make sense if they are productised or reuse existing components.

### Cited Findings
**AI integration:**
- Luca Sammarco: a pilot for one use case, such as automatic reading of incoming invoices using Azure OCR, Claude and the TeamSystem API, costs **4.000-10.000 € setup plus 150-400 €/month** and takes **3-5 weeks**. Wider integration across several use cases costs 12.000-30.000 € setup. RPA on legacy gestionali without APIs costs 10.000-25.000 € setup plus 400-1.000 €/month. — [Luca Sammarco, Integrare AI nel gestionale 2026](https://lucasammarco.com/blog/integrare-ai-gestionale-aziendale)
- The same author: custom AI on an existing Italian gestionale (TeamSystem or Zucchetti) costs **5.000-25.000 € one-off plus 200-1.500 €/month**. — [Luca Sammarco, AI gestionali italiani TeamSystem vs Zucchetti 2026](https://lucasammarco.com/blog/ai-gestionali-italiani-teamsystem-zucchetti-2026)
- The same author: document and invoice automation projects cost 3.000-40.000 € setup plus 100-2.500 €/month (OCR, LLM, workflow and database). — [Luca Sammarco, Automazione documentale](https://lucasammarco.com/blog/automazione-documentale-aziendale). ### Inferences
- **Completo + AI (13.900 € + 249 €/month)**: the AI premium is +5.000 € one-off and +100 €/month for three AI functions (document drafting, email and invoice reading, chat Q&A). The market prices **one** such function at 4.000-10.000 € plus 150-400 €/month (Sammarco), so the premium is roughly a third to a half of the market rate. Pricing the AI uplift at about **8.000-12.000 €** (an AI tier of about 17.000-22.000 €) would still be competitive. The 249 €/month canone is in line with the market (150-400 €/month per use case) and comfortably covers typical API costs of about 30-120 €/month at small-PMI volumes. Set a fair-use cap on AI volume.
- **"Funzione AI" extra at 1.900 €** is below every published AI setup figure except the lowest entry-chatbot band (500-2.000 €). Suggested range: **2.500-4.000 €** per function, or keep 1.900 € only for templated functions that reuse an existing pipeline.
- **"App sul telefono" at 1.200 €** is far below standalone PWA prices (6.000-40.000 €). It is defensible **only as a PWA layer on the existing responsive web app** (installable, offline cache, push). Label it that way ("installabile sul telefono, PWA") so clients do not compare it with native app quotes. A native app would need its own quote.
- **"Migrazione dati" at 900 €** is about 2-3 dev-days at 300-450 €/day. That is plausible for a clean Excel or CSV migration, risky for legacy databases. Make the price conditional on data quality or source count, or quote "da 900 €".

### Gaps
- No Italian source prices AI features specifically as add-on modules to a custom gestionale (as opposed to AI on TeamSystem or Zucchetti, or standalone chatbots).
- No published Italian price for a PWA add-on to an existing web gestionale was found. All PWA figures are for standalone builds.
- No sourced figure for data migration was found.
